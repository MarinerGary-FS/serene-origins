BEFORE YOU JOIN

A few helpful answers.

Is this medical care?

No. Serene Origins membership is a peer support and education experience, not a medical provider or substitute for professional healthcare. Consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

Who is the community for?

People living with herpes at any stage who want education, connection, and a more structured support experience.

How do I get into the Discord community?

After checkout, LaunchPass provides the steps to connect your Discord account and receive the member permissions associated with your active membership.

What happens if I cancel?

Membership and Discord permissions are managed through LaunchPass. When your membership ends, access to the private member space may be removed automatically.

Does membership include the supplement?

Membership provides community access and education. The Serene Origins jar is a separate product.
