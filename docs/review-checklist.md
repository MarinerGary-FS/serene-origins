# Review Checklist — Levy Presentation

Use this checklist before presenting the redesign.

## Strategy
- Does the site clearly explain what Serene Origins is?
- Does the membership feel tangible?
- Does the flow feel intentional?
- Is the difference from the current site obvious?

## Messaging
- Is the headline sharp?
- Is the copy emotionally intelligent without being vague?
- Are there any risky medical claims?
- Does the trust language feel credible?

## UX
- Is the CTA visible early?
- Is the pricing clear?
- Is the path to payment simple?
- Does the user understand what happens after signup?

## Visual design
- Does it feel premium?
- Does it feel private and emotionally safe?
- Is there enough hierarchy and whitespace?
- Does mobile feel strong?

## Technical
- Are all CTA buttons working?
- Does Stripe link correctly?
- Are there any broken anchors or sections?
- Is performance acceptable?
- Are fonts, spacing, and components consistent?

## Presentation readiness
- Would Levy understand the strategic improvement immediately?
- Does the design look intentional enough to approve?
- Would this feel like a serious client-ready case study later?
