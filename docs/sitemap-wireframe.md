# Sitemap + Wireframe — Serene Origins

## Recommended site model
A focused landing page with possible footer links for legal/support pages.

## Main navigation
- About
- How It Works
- Membership
- FAQ
- Join Now

## Core page structure

### 1. Hero
**Purpose:** Immediate clarity and emotional relevance

**Must communicate:**
- who it is for
- what it helps with
- primary action

**Suggested content blocks:**
- headline
- subheadline
- primary CTA
- optional secondary anchor link
- subtle reassurance line

---

### 2. “You’re not imagining the weight of this” section
**Purpose:** Reflect pain and internal reality

**Possible content:**
- short intro paragraph
- 3–4 emotional pain cards
- language around confusion, shame, isolation, unpredictability

---

### 3. The Serene System
**Purpose:** Make the offer feel structured and unique

**Three pillar cards:**
1. Awareness
2. Stabilization
3. Support

Each card should explain what that pillar gives the user.

---

### 4. What’s included
**Purpose:** Make membership tangible

**Include items like:**
- private community access
- guided discussions or support structure
- educational resources
- practical lifestyle guidance
- a more grounded space to ask questions and connect

---

### 5. What happens after you join
**Purpose:** Remove friction and uncertainty

**Suggested step flow:**
1. Join through LaunchPass
2. Receive onboarding / access instructions
3. Enter the private member space
4. Begin with a clear next step

---

### 6. Experience preview
**Purpose:** Make the invisible product visible

**Can include:**
- Discord preview placeholders
- interface mockups
- “inside the experience” cards
- examples of category organization or support flows

---

### 7. Mission / founder trust section
**Purpose:** Build legitimacy and human connection

**Can include:**
- why the brand exists
- what makes the approach different
- values of discretion, clarity, and support

---

### 8. Pricing
**Purpose:** Present the offer simply and confidently

**Recommended layout:**
- one featured membership card
- $29/month
- short feature stack
- LaunchPass CTA
- “cancel anytime” if accurate

---

### 9. FAQ
**Purpose:** reduce objections

**Topics to cover:**
- Is this medical treatment?
- What do I get after joining?
- Is the community private?
- Is this for people newly diagnosed or long-term?
- How are checkout and Discord access handled?

---

### 10. Final CTA
**Purpose:** clean emotional close + action

**Goal:** End with calm confidence, not pressure

---

## Footer
- Privacy Policy
- Terms
- Contact / Support
- LaunchPass Discord access reassurance if appropriate
