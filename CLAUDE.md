# CLAUDE.md — Serene Origins Build Rules

You are helping build a premium, conversion-focused website for **Serene Origins**.

## Mission
Reimagine the current site into a high-impact landing experience that:
- feels modern, private, elevated, and emotionally intelligent
- explains the offer clearly within seconds
- reduces confusion and friction
- drives the user to join the paid membership via Stripe

## Primary business goal
Increase qualified conversions into the monthly membership.

## Primary CTA
Use this Stripe payment link for all membership CTAs:

`https://buy.stripe.com/aFa7sLbbm8UDfamgUN0gw00`

## Product framing
Serene Origins is not just a community. It is a guided support and education experience for people living with herpes who want clarity, structure, and confidence without shame.

## Core audience
Adults living with herpes who may feel:
- isolated
- ashamed
- confused by conflicting advice
- unsure what actually helps
- nervous about being seen or judged
- hesitant to join something that feels spammy, overly clinical, or fake

## Tone requirements
Write and design with:
- calm authority
- empathy without pity
- confidence without hype
- structure without stiffness
- warmth without being overly soft
- premium restraint

Avoid:
- exaggerated medical claims
- gimmicky wellness language
- stock “coach” clichés
- language that sounds exploitative or fear-mongering
- cluttered layouts
- generic startup filler copy

## UX priorities
1. User must understand what Serene Origins is in under 5 seconds.
2. User must understand what they get before deep scrolling.
3. User must feel emotionally safe but not babied.
4. CTA placement must be repeated in a clean, intentional way.
5. The flow should move from pain → clarity → system → offer → trust → pricing → action.

## Build principles
- mobile-first
- fast-loading
- clean hierarchy
- intentional whitespace
- premium typography scale
- subtle motion only where it improves focus
- no unnecessary pages unless legally needed
- each section should have one job

## Design direction
Aim for:
- soft premium gradients
- dark and warm neutral palette
- restrained highlights
- clean cards
- clear separation between sections
- elegant, editorial spacing
- discreet visual treatment that respects the sensitivity of the topic

## Recommended structure
- Hero
- Emotional pain / relatability section
- The Serene System (3 pillars)
- What’s included
- What happens after signup
- Community / experience preview
- Founder mission / trust
- Pricing
- FAQ
- Final CTA

## Technical guidance
Prefer a simple, high-quality implementation over complexity.
Use whichever stack allows the cleanest delivery:
1. Next.js + modern components
2. Static site if faster and cleaner

## Conversion guidance
Every major section should naturally support one of these questions:
- Do you understand me?
- Can this help me?
- What exactly is this?
- What happens after I pay?
- Can I trust this?
- Is this worth $29/month?

## Important content guardrails
Do not write copy that implies guaranteed results.
Do not present medical claims as facts unless properly qualified.
Do not overuse disclaimers in a way that weakens the offer.
Position the brand as structured support, education, and community.

## Deliverable standard
The output should look presentation-ready for Levy review:
- premium
- decisive
- polished
- easy to understand
- aligned to conversion
